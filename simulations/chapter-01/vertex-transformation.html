Vertex transformation: one vertex, several coordinate spaces

A model's vertex positions start in local space. The rendering pipeline re-expresses those positions until they become pixel locations.

Key idea: the triangle is not changing into a different object. Its coordinates are described relative to a different frame of reference at each stage.

Object space

Vertices are stored relative to the model's own origin. A reusable cube or triangle is normally authored in this local space.
