Graphics Processors: who does the rendering work?

The CPU describes a scene and sends commands. The GPU performs rendering operations independently while the CPU continues with other work.

Key idea: a 3D program does not usually draw pixels itself. It submits vertices, textures, shaders, and commands to a graphics API; the driver translates those commands for the GPU.
