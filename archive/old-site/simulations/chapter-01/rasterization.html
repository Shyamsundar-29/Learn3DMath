Rasterization and fragment operations

This stage converts a projected triangle into work at pixel locations.

Key idea: rasterization creates fragments, not final pixels. A fragment has a screen location and interpolated data such as depth, color, and texture coordinates. Only fragments that pass the tests may write to the image buffer.
1. Face cullingSkip polygons facing away from the camera.
2. RasterizationFind covered pixel locations and create fragments.
3. Fragment shadingCalculate a final color from textures or lighting.
4. Depth testKeep only the fragment nearer than the depth buffer value.
5. Image bufferWrite or blend the surviving fragment's color.
